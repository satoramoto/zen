# Trigonometry course ownership

The full manuscript and executable course scenarios moved to human-free in
`move-trig-book-to-human-free`. The canonical `trig-course` specification now lives
in the zen-flow store at `openspec/specs/trig-course/spec.md`, alongside
`site-books` and `compendium-package`. The previous Zen-local specification was
retired with approval during migration archive; its history remains in Git and
`openspec/changes/archive/2026-11-08-trig-course/`.

Read human-free's `docs/books.md` for the route and verification commands, and
Zen's `docs/compendium-package.md` for reusable public imports.
